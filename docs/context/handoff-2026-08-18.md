## Where things stand

**Working and verified live:**
- **Touch interaction model** (`--map touch`, the camera default): the screen
  is a fixed-point surface under the hand, not a mouse. Pinch = tap.
- **Dynamic reach box**: appears centred on the palm wherever the hand shows
  up, shaped like the real display (Quartz-queried 1512x982), sized to the
  hand's apparent size (same physical box at any distance), and it FOLLOWS
  overshoot — crossing an edge slides the box, reversal responds instantly.
  Calibrated by `reach corners` (10s); stored in `camera_tuning.json`
  (gitignored — machine-local).
- **Jitter fix, audited**: root cause was 1-euro `beta` unscaled under box
  zoom. Fixed (`beta /= zoom`), gain boost made zoom-aware. A 3-agent
  audit swarm verified the numbers; the test view has a live REST JITTER
  meter (target <2px RMS on the dot).
- **Kinematic pinch gate**: clicks only latch below 150mm/s physical hand
  speed (RIDS/UIST 2022 rationale) — killed the 24-of-28 mid-flight phantom
  presses.
- **Command fixes**: busy gated on ring progress; click-pinch/fist shadows
  stop transition poses arming mission/dictate; V-sign joins ILY in
  label-trust routing so lone left-hand paste works; back-of-hand poses are
  legal (predicates never read palm orientation).
- **IMU over the web path**: the Safari page streams DeviceMotion at 5Hz via
  a WebRTC datachannel; the Mac EMA-tracks gravity and warns when the STAND
  IS BUMPED (>1.5 m/s^2 off gravity).
- **LeapDepth iOS companion** (`ios/LeapDepth/`): Swift/AVFoundation app on
  the iPhone 17 Pro streaming synchronized RGB + metric depth (LPD1/TCP,
  `python -m leapinput.phonedepth` receives). Proven live: real millimetre
  readings from front TrueDepth at 640x480. Role for now: occasional
  CALIBRATION INSTRUMENT, not the daily driver (see plan doc).

## THE OPEN BUG (start here next session)

Live test via the menu-bar path showed **phantom holds/selects at rest** —
"holds without pinching, selects randomly". Diagnosis: the pose thresholds
in `camera_tuning.json` were fitted on the OLD camera geometry; from the
stand's angle a relaxed hand foreshortens into "pinched". The kinematic gate
blocks moving phantoms but not resting ones, and in touch mode every latch
selects.

**The fix is ready and takes ~3 minutes:**

    .venv/bin/python -m leapinput.calibrate capture --source phone

(new this session: `--source phone` calibrates through the stand's own
camera; the fitter now PRESERVES the reach/setup fields while refitting pose
thresholds — that preservation bug is pinned by a test.) Follow the guided
poses, especially "apart, ready to pinch" vs "pinch" — that boundary is the
phantom-click boundary. Then verify in the dry-run front end:

    .venv/bin/python -m leapinput.reach test --source phone

Watch: REST JITTER line (green under 2px), no CLICK label without a real
pinch, `would press Cmd+V` on a left ✌️. Then go live: menu-bar ✋ → Turn on.

## Daily-use cheatsheet

- Menu bar ✋ Turn on / Turn off (always-on, phone source). CLI:
  `scripts/leapctl on --source phone` / `off` / `status`.
- Phone page: `https://<mac-ip>:8443/<token>` (bookmark; token persisted).
  LeapDepth app must be CLOSED for the page to get the camera.
- Pause in-band: hold 🤟 ~1.5s (chime). Vocabulary: point = place cursor,
  pinch = tap, pinch-hold+move = drag, open hand = park; free left hand:
  pinch-hold = Cmd+C, ✌️ = Cmd+V, 🤟 = Enter.
- Recalibrate the zone anytime: `reach corners --source phone` (10s).
  Hand size (metric readouts): `reach hand --span-mm <ruler mm>`.
  Inspect: `reach show`. Modes: `reach anchor palm|fixed`, `--map relative`.
